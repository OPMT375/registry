# Pitch: Make the Case to Switch Suppliers

**Monday, Dec 21 (final exam slot) · 12 minutes + 5 minutes of Q&A · Rehearsal Tuesday, Dec 15**

Bob plays a Minnesota buyer at the link downstream of yours. That buyer is happy enough with the current supplier (the incumbent). Your job is to get them to switch.

Put your deck in this folder (`pitch/deck.pdf`, or a link to Google Slides or PowerPoint in this file). Keep your speaker notes here too.

## The 12 minutes

| Part | Who | Time | What to cover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The buyer's pain | Team | ~2 min | What the incumbent's performance costs the buyer, in their terms (money, time, risk). |
| 2. Proof, pillar by pillar | Each teammate | 2 min each | Your claim, your metric against the incumbent's (from the dashboard), and the real company that did it. |
| 3. Switching risk | Team | ~1 min | What makes switching hard for the buyer, and how your firm makes it easy. |
| 4. The ask | Team | ~1 min | Exactly what you want the buyer to commit to: a trial order, a contract, a pilot. |

Everyone answers at least one question in Q&A.
